# Methodology v1.5.0-preview.2: Singles Shadow, Sealed Deferred

This version supersedes `1.5.0-preview.1` for new private collector calculations. It
does not alter the existing v1.4 price-leader series or any prior shadow artifact.

## Enabled scope

The ten singles collector indexes remain enabled with the preview.1 contract:

- every eligible `(cm_product_id, variant_key)` is included;
- latest positive Cardmarket `avg30` must be at least EUR 10;
- `avg30` is the only canonical selection and valuation field;
- no listing-price fallback, ranking, target count, deduplication, quota, or
  concentration cap;
- equal weight at monthly rebalance, then drifting weights;
- `avg1` remains a diagnostic Trading Activity Proxy, never transaction count,
  volume, liquidity, or a membership gate;
- the series remains private until the 60-day, two-composition, audit, and human
  cutover controls pass.

## Deferred scope

All ten sealed definitions remain reserved but are not calculable or publishable.
Their source status is `rolling_sold_price_unavailable` because the Cardmarket bulk
files do not populate non-single `avg1`, `avg7`, or `avg30` fields.

The EUR 30 sealed threshold is retained as the intended future economic floor. It is
not evaluated against lifetime `SELL`, `trend`, current listings, or scraped page
values. A future source decision requires a new methodology version and starts a
separate history.

## Version and output controls

```text
methodology_version = 1.5.0-preview.2
methodology_state   = private_shadow
public_alias        = disabled
splice_with_prior   = false
```

Enabled outputs continue under the methodology-versioned private prefix. Deferred
indexes produce no index history, composition, contribution, or diagnostics bundle;
the aggregate methodology audit alone records their deferred status.
