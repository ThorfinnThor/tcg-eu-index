# Methodology v1.5.0-preview.1: Collector Market Shadow

Historical preview note: new private calculations move to `1.5.0-preview.2`. Preview.1
remains reproducible, but its sealed family is not publishable because the Cardmarket
bulk files do not populate rolling sold-price fields for non-singles.

Status: implementation contract for a private shadow series

This specification is controlled by ADR 016. It does not change the active v1.4 public preview configuration.

## 1. Purpose

The Collector Market indexes measure the price development of the complete eligible Cardmarket market above a fixed collector threshold for each supported game. They do not claim to represent an average collector's holdings or the economic size of the market.

Separate series continue to be required for singles and sealed products because their price formation and stale-data behaviour differ.

## 2. Source and claims

The source is Cardmarket's daily public product catalogue and price guide.

- `avg1`: average sale price over the preceding day
- `avg7`: average sale price over the preceding seven days
- `avg30`: average sale price over the preceding 30 days

These are aggregated sale-price fields. The source does not expose the underlying `idArticle`, order, sale quantity, condition, language, or transaction timestamp. The methodology therefore does not claim true traded volume, order-book liquidity, market capitalization, or portfolio replicability.

## 3. Index families

### 3.1 Collector Singles

```text
universe = singles
minimum latest avg30 = EUR 10
membership = all eligible product variants
target size = none
```

### 3.2 Collector Sealed

```text
universe = sealed
minimum latest avg30 = EUR 30
membership = all eligible product variants
target size = none
```

### 3.3 Existing price leaders

Methodology v1.4 Top-500 singles and Top-100 sealed series remain separate historical and potentially public price-leader products. No v1.5 collector calculation overwrites their codes, files, or history.

## 4. Constituent identity

The stable identity is:

```text
(cm_product_id, variant_key)
```

Every variant is assessed independently. A Cardmarket product can contribute both foil and nonfoil variants when both pass all rules. No variant is selected merely because it is more expensive than another variant of the same product.

The following mechanisms are prohibited in the collector headline index:

- fixed constituent counts
- ranking by absolute price after eligibility
- price-band seat quotas
- rarity, era, set, product-type, metacard, or expansion quotas
- metacard and expansion concentration caps
- shortfall-filling algorithms
- price or market-cap weighting

## 5. Rebalance eligibility

Eligibility is evaluated using only observations strictly before the effective date.

### 5.1 Official eligibility

A variant is eligible when all conditions hold:

```text
correct game and product universe
seasoning_days >= 90
history_days >= 60
valuation_observation_ratio >= 0.80
selection_price_observation_ratio >= 0.80
suspect_zero_ratio <= 0.05
latest avg30 >= family threshold
latest avg30 > 0
```

The family threshold is EUR 10 for singles and EUR 30 for sealed.

### 5.2 Shadow eligibility

Before 60 days exist, a private shadow may reduce only `history_days`. The first shadow composition requires at least seven observable source days. Every relaxation is written to the selection manifest. Official readiness continues to require 60 days and is not affected by the shadow rule.

### 5.3 Threshold timing

The latest `avg30` before the selection cut-off determines threshold eligibility. A constituent remains in the basket between monthly rebalances even if its later `avg30` crosses the threshold.

No threshold buffer is used in preview. If turnover later proves excessive, any entry/exit buffer requires a separate methodology version.

## 6. Data quality and activity

### 6.1 Data Quality Score

The current score is renamed without changing its initial formula:

```text
data_quality_score
= 0.50 * valuation_observation_ratio
+ 0.30 * price_update_frequency
+ 0.20 * inverse_dispersion
```

Hard eligibility gates remain separate from the score. The score is diagnostic and must not be labelled liquidity.

For v1.5, a valuation observation is a positive canonical valuation field and a selection-price observation is a positive canonical reference field. Both fields are `avg30` in this version. `selection_price_observation_ratio` measures source-price availability; it does not require the variant to have remained above the EUR 10 or EUR 30 membership threshold throughout the lookback. Threshold membership is determined only by the latest positive `avg30` before the effective date as specified in section 5.3.

The quality denominator contains valid archived game snapshots in the lookback, including a snapshot whose payload is byte-identical to the previous day. Archive gaps are not fabricated. Unchanged snapshots are excluded separately from the Trading Activity Proxy denominator so feed repetition cannot be mistaken for a fresh activity signal.

### 6.2 Trading Activity Proxy

For an effective date `t`, observable source days are valid game price-guide snapshots in `[t-60, t)`. Archive gaps and snapshots explicitly marked `unchanged_from_previous` are excluded from the denominator.

```text
activity_days_60(i, t)
= observable source days where avg1(i, d) > 0

activity_ratio_60(i, t)
= activity_days_60(i, t) / observable_days_60(game, t)
```

The shadow output also records the last positive `avg1` date and days since that signal.

Repeated equal positive `avg1` values on successive observable rows are retained as a private diagnostic. They count as positive proxy days under the formula above, but their repeat rate is reported during calibration because the public source cannot reveal whether an equal value represents new sales at the same average or a repeated rolling aggregate.

For v1.5.0-preview.1:

```text
activity_gate_enabled = false
```

The proxy must first be tested for repeated rolling values, null/zero semantics, game differences, and unchanged-feed behaviour. Enabling a hard minimum later requires a version bump and a documented calibration result.

## 7. Reference and valuation price

Canonical v1.5.0-preview.1 fields:

```text
reference_price_field = avg30
valuation_price_field = avg30
price_fallback = none
```

`price_low` remains available only in private source diagnostics. It is never substituted for a missing aggregated sale-price field.

An alternate private `avg7` calculation may be compared for responsiveness, missingness, carry-forward frequency, volatility, and cap usage. It is not the canonical series.

## 8. Rebalance calendar

```text
cadence = monthly
selection_as_of = last observable source day before effective date
effective_date = first observable source day of the calendar month
```

The first private shadow composition may start after seven observable source days. Thereafter membership is frozen until the next effective date.

Daily eligible-universe snapshots are private diagnostics and are not exported as membership changes.

## 9. Weighting and calculation

### 9.1 Rebalance weights

For `N_t` eligible constituents on a rebalance date:

```text
w(i, 0) = 1 / N_t
```

If `N_t = 0`, no index value is calculated and the series reports `empty_eligible_universe`.

The first non-empty effective date is initialized at 1000 and receives no return from before the series inception. At later monthly rebalances, equal target weights are applied before the effective-date calculation. Each basket member uses its frozen `selection_as_of` `avg30` as the prior valuation, including newly added or returning variants.

### 9.2 Daily return

For the used constituent return `r(i,t)` and the prior-day weight:

```text
R(t) = sum_i [w(i,t-1) * r(i,t)]
Index(t) = Index(t-1) * (1 + R(t))
w(i,t) = w(i,t-1) * (1 + r(i,t)) / (1 + R(t))
```

Weights therefore drift between rebalances and reset to equal target weights only when the monthly membership is established.

### 9.3 Component return cap

The initial shadow cap remains:

```text
-0.25 <= used constituent daily return <= 0.25
```

Raw and used returns, capped constituent count, and capped weight share are retained. The cap is a provisional data-quality control and must be calibrated before official publication.

## 10. Missing data

For a constituent without a valid positive valuation price:

1. carry the last valid price for at most five consecutive calendar days;
2. use a zero constituent return while carried;
3. retain its weight rather than renormalizing other constituents;
4. after five days, mark it `suspended_stale`, retain the last valuation, and continue a zero return;
5. expose carried and suspended weight shares in quality outputs.

The five-day limit is measured as calendar-day age from the last valid valuation: ages one through five are carried and age six is suspended. An unchanged whole-market snapshot does not reset that date, and an already suspended constituent remains suspended. Fresh observations after carry or suspension are compared with the retained last valid valuation and remain subject to the component return cap.

A catalogue deletion or prolonged suspension requires an exceptional-deletion rule before official launch. The private preview may retain the suspended constituent until the next monthly rebalance.

## 11. Output identity and history

All derived objects are namespaced by methodology version and data state:

```text
derived/indexes/{methodology_version}/{data_state}/{index_code}/
derived/diagnostics/{methodology_version}/{index_code}/
```

Each output contains:

```text
series_id = "{index_code}:{methodology_version}:{data_state}"
methodology_version
data_state
selection_as_of
effective_date
source manifest hashes
calculation commit SHA
```

The v1.5 series begins at 1000 on its own first effective date. No v1.4 and v1.5 values are concatenated.

## 13. Shadow calibration requirements

Before public approval, compare at minimum:

- constituent count by game and month
- threshold entrants and exits
- distribution and missingness of `avg1`, `avg7`, and `avg30`
- repeated `avg1` signals across unchanged snapshots
- Data Quality Score distribution
- monthly turnover
- largest target and drifted constituent weights
- capped, carried, and suspended weight shares
- `avg30` canonical versus `avg7` alternate return, volatility, and drawdown
- overlap and return correlation with v1.4 price-leader series

There is no target-size launch gate. Readiness requires source-history integrity, deterministic calculation, acceptable quality metrics, activity-proxy validation, and human review.

## 14. Public cutover controls

The v1.5 shadow remains private until all conditions hold:

1. at least 60 observable source days for the index;
2. verified `avg1` semantics or an explicit decision to keep activity diagnostic-only;
3. reproducible selection and calculation hashes;
4. reviewed cap, carry, suspension, turnover, and concentration reports;
5. public schema and UI deployed atomically with the dataset;
6. explicit human approval of the methodology version;
7. a manual alias/cutover action.

The existing public v1.4 preview remains available until that cutover. A failed v1.5 shadow run cannot modify its files.
