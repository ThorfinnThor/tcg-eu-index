# Methodology v1.5 S4: Calibration Audit

## Outcome

The private aggregate audit in GitHub Actions run 33262797253 evaluated methodology
`1.5.0-preview.1` against the normalized Cardmarket archive through 2026-08-29. It
covered all ten games and all twenty collector index definitions without publishing
raw prices or product identities.

The public cutover remains blocked. The singles family can continue in private shadow
with `avg30` as its canonical sold-price field and `avg1` as a diagnostic Trading
Activity Proxy. The sealed family cannot be calculated from true sold prices in the
current source: every sealed row has null or non-positive `avg1`, `avg7`, and `avg30`.
All ten sealed compositions are consequently empty.

Cardmarket announced that non-singles were added to its downloadable price-guide
options in October 2024, and the official download page exposes one price-guide file
per game. The archived files do include products classified as sealed, but their
sold-price rolling averages are not populated. Current listing fields therefore must
not be substituted for `avg30`.

The correlations for One Piece and Riftbound are not decision-grade: they are based
on only 13 and 11 overlapping return days, respectively. Their divergence is retained
as an anomaly for the 60-day rerun. It does not justify replacing canonical `avg30`
with `avg7` now.

The counterfactual activity candidate gate passes 100% of currently eligible singles
in every game. Combined with the high repeated-positive `avg1` rates measured in S2
and complete absence of sealed signals, this confirms that `avg1` must remain a
diagnostic proxy rather than membership, liquidity, transaction-count, or volume data.

## Decision

1. Keep `avg30` canonical for collector singles.
2. Keep `avg1` diagnostic-only and do not enable an activity hard gate.
3. Do not use listing-price fallback for sealed products.
4. Keep every v1.5 route and output private; do not switch public aliases.
5. Require a new preview methodology/source decision before sealed publication.
6. Repeat the complete audit after at least 60 observable days and two monthly
   compositions.

## Versioned follow-up

The Sol follow-up resolves the S4 blocker in `1.5.0-preview.2` by formally deferring
the sealed family. The loader records its source status, the engine rejects sealed
calculation, and the aggregate audit reports deferred definitions without presenting
them as valid zero-member indexes. Singles remain unchanged and private.

GitHub Actions run `33264761077` executed the corrected audit against the same real R2
archive through 2026-08-29. It reported ten enabled singles indexes and ten deferred
sealed indexes. The decision status was
`preliminary_complete_with_deferred_family`; it requested neither preview.3 nor an
additional pre-publication methodology correction. The artifact remained
aggregate-only and contained no raw prices or product identities.

With the methodology correction complete, the next work package uses **Luna** for L4
CI, regression, and documentation synchronization. Sealed can be re-enabled only by a
later Sol-reviewed methodology version with a stable rolling sold-price source.
